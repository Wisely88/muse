# 🎬 P1-3：连续实测视频分镜提示词（5段镜头 · 恐龙宇宙延续篇）

> **主题选定**：《雨夜赛博机械霸王龙追踪》（Cyberpunk Jurassic Rain: Mecha-Rex Tracking）  
> **视觉定位**：完美承接上一期 44 秒恐龙精剪短片，将侏罗纪远古恐龙与未来赛博朋克工业机械融合，视觉张力拉满！  
> **技术规格**：16:9 宽画幅 | 24fps 电影帧率 | 7–10 秒/镜头 | 锁定外观与环境连续性约束

---

## 🔒 全局锁定约束 (Global Continuity & Negative Constraints)

### 【连续性锁定 (Continuity Lock)】
> `Subject: A massive bipedal bio-mechanical Tyrannosaurus Rex ("Mecha-Rex"), gunmetal scarred carbon-titanium armor plating, exposed glowing cyan hydraulic conduits, dual ocular cyan optics with horizontal scanning reticle, jagged reinforced metallic teeth.`  
> `Environment: Drenched futuristic neo-Tokyo industrial alleyway, heavy volumetric rain, puddles reflecting magenta and electric blue holographic neon billboards, dense steam venting from sewer grates, dark asphalt cobblestones.`  
> `Color & Optics: Shot on Arri Alexa Mini LF, 35mm anamorphic widescreen lens, horizontal blue streak lens flares, cinematic dark moody lighting with rich shadow contrast, photorealistic textures.`

### 【负向约束 (Negative Constraints)】
> `Negative Prompt: morphing body parts, extra limbs, extra heads, distorted anatomy, cartoonish textures, CGI rubber skin, daytime sunlight, blurry motion blur, sudden scene changes, low resolution, 3D video game artifacts.`

## 🎥 5 段连续镜头脚本与地道好莱坞英文 Prompt

### 镜头 1 (Shot 01 - 7秒)：巨兽现身 · 地面震颤低机位起幅
- **景别机位**：低角度仰拍（Low-angle ground-level establishing tracking shot）
- **镜头运动**：贴地向后缓慢拉远，震动水洼微观特写
- **英文 Prompt (可直接复制)**：
```text
An extreme low-angle ground-level establishing shot in a drenched neo-Tokyo cyberpunk alley at midnight. Torrential rain pours relentlessly onto glistening black asphalt. A gigantic three-toed mechanical dinosaur foot clad in scarred gunmetal titanium steps heavily into a deep puddle, sending concentric shockwaves and crystalline water splashes outward. The camera gently tracks backward along the pavement as steam violently vents from sewer grates. In the distant rain haze, the towering silhouette of the Mecha-Rex looms, its dual cyan optical visors igniting with electric intensity. Shot on 35mm anamorphic lens, Arri Alexa, cinematic high-contrast lighting, neon blue and magenta reflections on wet pavement, photorealistic 8k. Native audio: Heavy thunderous metallic footstep stomp, massive water splash, deep bass rumble, pouring rain, distant electronic siren wail. --ar 16:9
```

---

### 镜头 2 (Shot 02 - 8秒)：跟焦推进 · 机械脊椎与骨骼液压细节
- **景别机位**：过肩斜侧中景跟拍（Over-the-shoulder medium tracking shot）
- **镜头运动**：摄影机以电影级推轨速度伴随巨兽前行，平滑横移
- **英文 Prompt (可直接复制)**：
```text
A cinematic medium tracking shot smoothly following alongside the flank of the bio-mechanical Mecha-Rex as it stalks through the narrow neon-lit street. Rain streams down its reinforced carbon-fiber scales and ribbed titanium spine. Exposed cyan hydraulic conduits pulse with liquid coolant light under the dark metal plating. The mechanical tail sways with heavy, natural momentum, grazing a rusted fire escape and kicking off flying orange sparks. Wet overhead neon signboards cast flickering green and crimson glares across its scarred armor. Photorealistic cinematic grading, Panavision anamorphic lens flare, shallow depth of field, sharp focus, 8k uhd. Native audio: Heavy hydraulic servo whirring, pressurized steam hissing, metallic armor clanks, steady rhythmic footsteps splashing in puddles, howling wind. --ar 16:9
```

### 镜头 3 (Shot 03 - 8秒)：正面锁定 · 巨头俯瞰与机械吐息
- **景别机位**：正面推拉特写（Frontal slow push-in close-up shot）
- **镜头运动**：镜头迎面缓缓推进，直面恐龙巨颚
- **英文 Prompt (可直接复制)**：
```text
A cinematic front-facing close-up shot slowly pushing in toward the terrifying head of the Mecha-Rex. Rainwater cascades over its armored cranial horns and razor-sharp metal teeth. The predator lowers its massive skull toward the lens, breathing out dense plumes of white steam from glowing nostrils. Its glowing turquoise ocular lens dilates and recalibrates with mechanical clicks, illuminating the raindrops in sharp pinpoint bokeh. Warm neon light from storefronts behind backlights its menacing silhouette with dramatic rim lighting. Arri Alexa Master Prime optics, crisp micro-details, volumetric rain mist, Hollywood blockbuster realism. Native audio: Menacing low-frequency sub-bass guttural growl, rhythmic mechanical clicks of targeting optics, loud pressurized steam venting, rain pattering on steel skull. --ar 16:9
```

### 镜头 4 (Shot 04 - 9秒)：猎物对峙 · 探员雨夜驻足与视线交汇
- **景别机位**：双人视线交汇逆向推进（Reverse tracking two-shot reveal）
- **镜头运动**：镜头从巨兽腿部越过，推向雨中街角的孤独探员
- **英文 Prompt (可直接复制)**：
```text
A dynamic cinematic medium two-shot in the rainy cyberpunk crossroad. In the foreground, the massive claw of the Mecha-Rex stands locked on the asphalt. In the midground, a lone human detective wearing a drenched dark trench coat and glowing blue optical visor stands frozen under an illuminated phone booth, looking up fearfully at the beast. The camera slowly cranes up from behind the detective's shoulder, capturing the colossal height disparity as neon light reflections ripple across the drenched ground between them. Atmospheric chiaroscuro lighting, volumetric rain haze, cinematic 35mm film stock, high dynamic range. Native audio: Tense atmospheric ambient drone, distant police sirens, raindrops drumming on phone booth glass, heavy panting breaths, sudden loud metallic thud. --ar 16:9
```
